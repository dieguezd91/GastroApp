# Purchase invoices with Argentine VAT (IVA)
Branch `feat/purchase-invoice-vat` (from `main` b2b3b1e). No push (main deploys to Render). Spanish UI, English code/docs. Scope: purchases only (L2 "Solo compras"); no ARCA electronic invoicing. T1 changes the persisted format (Risk: item 1) and the meaning of the "last price" used for costing (item 3): own commit, harness, independent verification.

## Specs
- S1 (L1 "el negocio como tal no existe, esta aplicacion es abierta a todo tipo de emprendedor gastronomico"): each installation stores its own business tax condition (condición frente al IVA): `Responsable Inscripto`, `Monotributista` or `Exento`. An admin-only screen "Datos fiscales" (`/settings/fiscal`) sets it; the service re-checks a live admin. While it is not set, registering a purchase invoice is rejected with "Configurá la condición frente al IVA del negocio en Datos fiscales antes de registrar facturas." and the invoice screen links to the settings. "Reiniciar datos" clears it.
- S2: a supplier stores CUIT and tax condition (same three values). Both are required when creating or editing. The CUIT has 11 digits and a valid check digit ("El CUIT no es válido."), may be typed with dashes, is stored as 11 digits, and is unique among suppliers ("Ya existe un proveedor con ese CUIT."). A supplier missing either value (legacy data) is listed as "Datos fiscales incompletos" and cannot be used on a new invoice until it is completed.
- S3 (L1 "Hay que discriminar el tipo de factura"): every new invoice records its type, derived from both conditions and shown read-only: supplier Monotributista or Exento → C; supplier Responsable Inscripto and business Responsable Inscripto or Monotributista → A; supplier Responsable Inscripto and business Exento → B. The service rejects any other type with "Un proveedor {condición} debe emitirte factura {tipo esperado}.". Factura M is not offered (removed by RG 5762/2025 since 2025-12-01); an A invoice may carry the optional legend "OPERACIÓN SUJETA A RETENCIÓN" or "PAGO EN CBU INFORMADA".
- S4 (L1 "aclarar el iva donde sea necesario"): on factura A the typed price is net (sin IVA) and each line has a VAT rate from 0%, 2,5%, 5%, 10,5%, 21%, 27%; the invoice shows neto gravado and IVA per rate (IVA = neto × alícuota, rounded to cents per rate). On factura B the typed price is final (IVA incluido), lines have no rate, and the invoice may record the informative "IVA contenido" printed under RG 5614/2024 (≥ 0 and lower than the invoice total). On factura C the typed price is final and no VAT field is accepted. Invalid VAT data is rejected and nothing is stored.
- S5 (L1 "Si quiero registrar"; L2 selected "Percepción de IVA", "Percepción de IIBB", "Impuestos internos / otros"): an invoice may list other taxes, each with amount > 0: Percepción de IVA (factura A only), Percepción de IIBB (requires a jurisdiction), Impuestos internos, Otros tributos (requires a description). They add to the invoice total and are not part of any ingredient unit cost (documented limitation).
- S6: the ingredient unit cost used by recipes and shown as "último precio" is: the net price when the business is Responsable Inscripto and the invoice is A (IVA is crédito fiscal); net × (1 + alícuota) for an A invoice when the business is not Responsable Inscripto; the typed price for B, C and legacy invoices. Changing the business condition recalculates stored recipe costs.
- S7 (L3 "Solo mis datos locales de prueba"): invoices stored before this change load unchanged, shown as "Tipo no informado · precio final", and are never reinterpreted: their price is their cost. Only this machine's `data.json` drops its two test invoices and the stock they added (Garrón back to 0).
- S8: the invoice list shows each invoice's type and, for A, neto, IVA and other taxes; docs (`Docs/Architecture/Purchasing.md`, IMPLEMENTED_FEATURES) and on-screen help describe the rules.
